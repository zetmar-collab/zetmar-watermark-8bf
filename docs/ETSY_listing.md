### Description

**Sign your photos in seconds – no more building layers by hand.**

"Signature (Watermark)" is a filter for **Adobe Photoshop** and **Adobe Photoshop Elements**. It places a text signature or an image logo on a separate transparent layer, so your original stays untouched and you control opacity and blend mode in the Layers panel, as usual.

**What it does**
- **Text signature:** any installed font (with a live preview in the list), bold, italic, multi-line, Unicode, letter spacing, line spacing.
- **Image signature:** PNG logo (with transparency), JPG or TIFF; scale, rotation, flip, and a "recolor to chosen color" option.
- **Uses your foreground color** by default.
- **Placement:** 9-point grid, margins in pixels or percent, fine offset, position relative to the selection.
- **Stroke** (outside, center, inside) and **emboss / bevel** (4 styles, raised or recessed, adjustable light angle and altitude).
- **Visibility 0–100 %**, plus the normal layer opacity and blend mode afterwards.
- **Live preview** of the whole photo with zoom and pan.
- **Presets:** save "Light signature bottom-right" or "Logo center 30 %" and reuse it in one click.
- **Ctrl+F** repeats the last settings on the next photo.
- Interface in **English and Polish**, light or dark theme.
- Handles photos up to 100 megapixels, RGB 8 and 16 bit.

**How it works (3 steps)**
1. Create an empty layer (Ctrl+Shift+N).
2. Filter → Zetmar → Signature (Watermark)…
3. Adjust the look and click OK.

**Installation:** the included installer detects the Adobe programs on your PC (Photoshop, Photoshop Elements) and installs the plug-in into the ones you tick. A manual option (copy one file) and a guide in English and Polish are included.

**Tested in:** Adobe Photoshop 2026 and Adobe Photoshop Elements 2026.

**Requirements**
- Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit (not available for macOS)
- Photoshop CC or Photoshop Elements 2020 or newer (64-bit)
- RGB images (8 or 16 bits per channel)

**What's in the download**

- `ZetmarWatermark_64.8bf` – the plug-in alone, for manual install
- `INSTALL_EN.txt` and `INSTRUKCJA_PL.txt`
